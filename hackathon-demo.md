# Hackathon Demo Runbook

## Primary demonstration

1. Open the dashboard and show the two panels: ISL Communication and Environmental Awareness.
2. Trigger a prerecorded or live fire-alarm audio clip.
3. Show the sound label, confidence, high-priority severity, and visual alert.
4. Demonstrate the vibration or visual fallback if supported by the device.
5. Trigger a selected ISL phrase such as “I need help” or “Please call my family.”
6. Show the raw recognized sequence and the corrected English output.
7. Press the confirmation control and show the final message to the hearing user.

## Backup plan

Keep prerecorded audio clips and prerecorded ISL examples available in the demo folder or as dashboard buttons. The demonstration must still work if microphone permissions, camera access, internet access, or model loading fails.

## Claims to avoid

Do not claim unrestricted ISL translation, guaranteed emergency detection, perfect accuracy, automatic emergency-service contact, or replacement of professional interpreters. Describe the system as a research prototype.

## Suggested 90-second pitch

“SunoSaathi is an assistive companion for Deaf and hard-of-hearing users. It solves two problems together: communicating with non-signers through selected ISL messages and receiving important environmental information that normally arrives through sound. When it detects a fire alarm, siren, horn, glass-breaking sound, or door knock, it provides a confidence-aware visual and haptic alert. When the user signs, the system converts the message into understandable English. We are building a focused prototype using public datasets and pretrained models, with reliability tested through latency, false-alarm, recognition, and usability measures.”
