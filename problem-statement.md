# Problem Statement

Deaf and hard-of-hearing users may face a two-way accessibility gap. They may be unable to communicate easily with non-signers because Indian Sign Language is not understood. At the same time, important environmental information may arrive through sound, including fire alarms, sirens, vehicle horns, doorbells, and glass breaking.

Most existing student solutions focus on either sign-to-text translation or isolated alert systems. SunoSaathi proposes a combined assistive prototype that helps users communicate outward through ISL and receive important environmental information inward through visual and haptic alerts.

## Target users

The primary users are Deaf and hard-of-hearing people who use or understand ISL. The secondary users are hearing people who need to understand an ISL message.

## Hackathon MVP

The prototype will support a limited ISL vocabulary and a small set of environmental sound classes. It will show confidence, classify alert severity, provide visual notifications, and offer optional speech output for the hearing conversation partner.
